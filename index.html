About the Firm

A disciplined approach to real estate investing.

Fieldworks Capital, LLC is an independent real estate investment firm managing capital on behalf of endowments, foundations, pension funds, family offices, and qualified individual investors.

We operate across multiple risk profiles — from core income-producing assets to opportunistic value-add plays — with a consistent focus on market-dominant locations, structural tailwinds, and downside protection.

Investment Strategy

Where and how we invest.

We pursue opportunities across four core property types in high-barrier-to-entry markets with durable demand fundamentals.

Multifamily

Workforce and market-rate apartment communities in supply-constrained Sun Belt and coastal markets. Focus on stable cash flow with embedded rent growth potential.

02

Industrial & Logistics

Core Development
03

Office & Mixed-Use

Core Opportunistic
04

Land & Agricultural

Raw land, entitled development sites, and agricultural holdings in high-growth corridors. Focus on long-term appreciation, entitlement value creation, and alternative land uses.

The Team

Experienced across cycles and asset classes.

Our investment professionals bring decades of combined expertise in acquisitions, asset management, and capital markets — united by a shared commitment to disciplined underwriting and long-term value creation.

Acquisitions & Underwriting

Our acquisitions team sources and underwrites investments across all four target property types. Deep market relationships and proprietary deal flow enable off-market transaction access in primary and secondary markets.

Asset Management & Operations

In-house asset management professionals oversee property operations, capital improvement programs, leasing strategy, and disposition planning — driving value creation at every stage of the hold period.

Capital Markets & Investor Relations

Our capital markets group manages LP relationships, fund structuring, debt placement, and investor reporting. We are committed to transparent communication and rigorous governance for all fund investors.
